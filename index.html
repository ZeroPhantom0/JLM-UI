About Us

More Than Just a Room — A Home

JLM Cozy Cabin provides affordable and comfortable accommodations in Ermita, Manila designed for individuals and groups looking for a clean, secure, and accessible place to stay. Whether for short-term or long-term stay, we offer a home-like environment with convenience and comfort.

Prime Location

Located at 1517 M.H. Del Pilar St., Ermita Manila corner Salas St., near major agencies, business centers, hospitals, and transport access.

Safe & Accessible

Close to US Embassy, Manning Agencies, Training Centers, Banks, Hospitals, Robinson Manila, and Manila Bay.

24/7 Open

No check-in time restrictions — guests can check in anytime.

Reservation Policy

All reservations at JLM Cozy Cabin are subject to room availability and the submission of complete and accurate reservation information.

Tenants must provide the required reservation details and select an available payment option before submitting their reservation. The available payment options may include a fifty percent (50%) down payment or full payment, depending on the applicable reservation requirements.

After submission, the reservation will be placed under Pending Verification status and will not be considered confirmed until reviewed by the Admin. The Admin will review the reservation details and submitted payment information before approving or rejecting the reservation.

An approved reservation will be confirmed by the system and the tenant will be notified of the reservation status. If the reservation does not meet the applicable requirements, the Admin may reject it and provide an appropriate reason.

Reservations may expire if the required requirements are not completed within the specified period or if the tenant fails to arrive on the scheduled move-in date, subject to the applicable reservation conditions.

Reservation cancellations, changes to reservation details, and room changes are subject to room availability, applicable policies, and Admin approval.

Upon move-in, tenants may be required to present a valid identification document and reservation confirmation for verification.

Payment and Billing Policy

Tenants are responsible for reviewing their billing information, payment amounts, due dates, and remaining balances shown in the system.

Required payments must be settled within the specified due date through the payment methods provided by JLM Cozy Cabin. Tenants must upload the required proof of payment when submitting a payment through the system.

Payments submitted through the system are subject to verification by the Admin. A payment will only be recorded as Verified once the submitted payment information and proof have been reviewed and confirmed.

Verified payments will be recorded in the tenant's payment history and an official payment receipt may be made available through the system. Pending or rejected payments will not be treated as verified payments.

Any discrepancy or concern regarding billing information or payment records should be reported to the Admin for review.

Failure to settle required payments within the applicable due date may result in the payment being marked as overdue. Additional charges, restrictions, or other actions may also be applied in accordance with JLM Cozy Cabin policies and the applicable boarding agreement.

JLM Cozy Cabin Privacy Policy

Learn how your personal information is collected, used, and protected.

Purpose of Data Collection

The personal information collected by JLM Cozy Cabin is used for the following purposes:

  • Verifying tenant identity
  • Processing reservations and room assignments
  • Managing billing and payments
  • Handling emergency and safety concerns
  • Sending important announcements and updates
  • Processing maintenance requests and tenant concerns
  • Maintaining accurate tenant records

We collect only the information that is necessary and proportionate to the purposes described above, in accordance with the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality under Republic Act No. 10173, or the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Use and Storage of Data

Your personal information is securely stored in the JLM Cozy Cabin management system and is accessible only to authorized personnel.

Personal information is kept only for as long as necessary for tenancy, reservations, billing, communication, and other legitimate purposes of the system.

JLM Cozy Cabin does not sell or use your personal information for unrelated commercial purposes. Information may only be shared when necessary for legitimate purposes or when required by law.

Access and Protection of Information

JLM Cozy Cabin implements appropriate technical, organizational, and physical security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Restricted physical access to records storage
  • Role-based system access controls
  • Encrypted storage of digital records
  • Regular review of access permissions

Only authorized management personnel — specifically the property owner, administrator, and designated staff — are permitted to access tenant personal information. Access is granted on a need-to-know basis only.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and affected data subjects within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by the Data Privacy Act.

Your Data Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

RIGHT TO INFORMATION

You have the right to know how your personal information is collected, used, and processed.

RIGHT TO ACCESS

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you.

RIGHT TO CORRECTION

You may request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

RIGHT TO ERASURE OR BLOCKING

You may request the deletion or blocking of your personal information when allowed by law.

RIGHT TO OBJECT

You may object to certain processing of your personal information when allowed by law.

RIGHT TO DATA PORTABILITY

Where applicable, you may request your personal information in a commonly used electronic format.

RIGHT TO LODGE A COMPLAINT

You may raise a privacy concern or file a complaint if you believe your data privacy rights have been violated.

Privacy Concerns and Contact

Tenants may contact JLM Cozy Cabin regarding questions, concerns, or requests related to their personal information. Tenants may also request access to or correction of their personal information, subject to applicable laws and verification requirements. Privacy-related concerns may be submitted through the contact information or communication channels provided by JLM Cozy Cabin.

Updates to the Privacy Policy

JLM Cozy Cabin may update this Privacy Policy / Data Privacy Notice when necessary to reflect changes in the system, services, data processing practices, or applicable requirements. Significant changes will be communicated through the system or other appropriate communication channels.
